## Rules

1. Do not paste confidential client data into AI tools unless the client and lecturer have explicitly permitted it.
2. Do not submit AI-generated code, diagrams, or text that you cannot explain.
3. Do not use AI to fabricate interviews, requirements, tests, commit history, or client feedback.
4. AI may support learning, brainstorming, debugging, and refactoring, but the team remains fully responsible for correctness, security, privacy, and maintainability.
